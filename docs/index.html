01 The idea

You already own two of the three parts.

A Plaud sells you three things: a recorder, transcription and AI notes. You already wear the recorder, and you already pay for the AI. Recly supplies the missing piece, for free, and keeps the original audio in your own Google Drive.

RecorderPlaud Note or NotePin: $159–$189The Galaxy Watch or Apple Watch you already wear: $0
Start recordingThe recorder's buttonGalaxy Watch: double press the home key. Apple Watch: the Action button on an Ultra, or one tap.

AI notesIn the Plaud appThe AI you already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex), through Recly's skills

02 How it works

From a press to your own AI, with nothing in between.

All six clients run the same fixed recording flow. Nothing waits on a Recly server, because there isn't one.

  1. 01 · Record

    Watch, phone, Mac or PC

    Tap record, bind it to the Galaxy Watch home key, or let the desktop notice a meeting and ask.

    00:12:34
  2. 02 · Store

    Your Google Drive

    The phone or PC uploads the original as it is. Watches hand off to the phone first. Offline, it waits and retries.

    recly/2026/2026-09/
  3. 03 · Transcribe

    On device, or your key

    After the original is safe, the transcript is written back next to it. Speakers are separated when the provider supports it.

    transcript.json
  4. 04 · Notes

    Your own AI agent

    Your agent reads the transcript from Drive and writes minutes, decisions or lecture notes into Notion.

    recly-notes · recly-notion

03 Why Recly

Record anywhere. Keep everything. Owe no one.

The recorder you already wear

A double press of the Galaxy Watch's home key starts recording; the watch hands the audio to your phone, and the phone does the rest. Apple Watch records in one tap, or from the Action button on an Ultra.

Meetings on your desktop

The Mac menu-bar app and the Windows tray app capture your mic and the other side of a Zoom, Teams or Meet call as separate tracks. They notice the meeting and ask first.

Your Drive is the only storage

Recordings go to a folder like recly/2026/2026-09/ in your own Google Drive, through drive.file, the narrowest permission Google offers.

Nothing gets lost

No network? Recordings wait and retry. The original is never deleted before Drive has confirmed the upload.

Files are the interface

Transcripts are plain files next to the recording, in a documented format. Any agent, script or app can read them.

Nothing covert

Recording is always visible, and there is no bot joining your call. No analytics, no crash reporting, no update pings.

05 Transcription

On the device, or with your own key.

Choose per device in Settings. On-device transcription never falls back to a cloud service you did not pick.

On device Default

New installs start here wherever the device has an engine. No audio goes to a speech service. It does not separate speakers.

External API Your key

Pick one of 14 providers (8 on iPhone) and enter your own API key on the device. The joined audio goes to that provider only, and speakers are separated when it supports it.

Off Upload only

Keep the original upload to Drive and skip transcription entirely.

06 Privacy

There is no Recly server to trust.

The only places data can go are your Google Drive, the transcription provider you chose, and your own paired watch or phone.

0Recly servers, databases or accounts
drive.fileThe narrowest Drive permission Google offers
0Analytics, crash reports or update pings
StepWhere it happensWhat leaves your device
RecordingYour watch, phone or desktopNothing. A watch hands the audio to your paired phone, and only there.
StorageYour Google DriveThe audio parts and a small metadata file, to your own account.
TranscriptionOn device, or a provider you chose with your own keyOn device: no audio to any speech service. External: the joined audio to the provider you selected. Results are written next to the recording in Drive.
NotesYour own AI agentThe agent reads the transcript from your Drive and writes to your Notion. Recly is not involved.
Settings, API keysYour deviceNothing is synced. Export moves configuration only; keys are entered separately on each device.

Every network path, with nothing left out, is in the privacy policy. Any change that adds a network call has to update that list first.

07 Notes

Notes are your agent's job, not a paid tier.

Recly's pipeline ends at the transcript on purpose. It ships skills for the AI you already use instead: Drive stays the archive the app writes, and the notes, with every later edit, live in your Notion.

recly-notes

Finds a recording, the latest or the one you name, reads its transcript and writes minutes, a decision log, interview or lecture notes, or a memo.

recly-notion

Keeps those notes in a "Recly Recordings" database in your Notion, one page per recording, and finds them again later.
