NAME
Recly: Record for Your AI

SUBTITLE
Record, transcribe, automate

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Record on iPhone and Apple Watch, save to your own Google Drive, and add optional transcription and webhook workflows.

DESCRIPTION
Record voice notes, interviews, and meetings with Recly. Record, save, and play audio on your device without signing in. Connect your own Google Drive when you want to upload recordings.

RECORD AND PLAY BACK
Record on iPhone or Apple Watch. Add titles to your recordings and play them back when you need them.

SAVE TO YOUR OWN GOOGLE DRIVE
Connect your Google account to upload recordings to your own Drive and access them from your devices.

OPTIONAL TRANSCRIPTION AND WORKFLOWS
Add speech-to-text using a supported third-party provider and your own API credentials. Configure optional webhooks to send recording metadata and Drive links to a destination you choose.

CONTROL YOUR CONNECTIONS
Recly does not create a user account. Google authorization is used to access your own Drive. Drive-based workflows, including transcription in this version, require a Google Drive connection. You can disconnect Drive in Settings and keep your recordings and settings.

Transcription provider availability varies by region. Third-party accounts may be required, and provider charges may apply.
