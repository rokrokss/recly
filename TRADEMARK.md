# Recly trademark policy

The AGPL covers **copyright only**. It gives you no right to use the project's name or brand, and
this page says what we do and do not allow instead.

## The marks

"Recly" as a word, the Recly app icon, and the Blueprint-style logo and wordmark are trademarks and
brand of Hyungrok Kim, registered or not.

## Allowed, no permission needed

- Saying, truthfully, that your fork or product is **based on Recly**, or was built from Recly's
  source.
- Linking to the project, writing about it, reviewing it, teaching it.
- Redistributing the **official builds** unmodified, with the name and icon they ship with.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a **modified** build under the name "Recly", or under a confusingly similar name — a
  variation, an abbreviation, a translation, or a compound such as "Recly Pro" or "OpenRecly" — in
  any app store, package repository, or download page.
- Using the Recly icon or logo, or a confusingly similar one, as the icon of a build that is not
  ours.
- Using the marks in a company, product, domain, or account name, or in any way that suggests the
  project endorses, supports, or is the source of what you are shipping.

Fork freely — the AGPL says you may. Just ship it under your own name and your own icon.

Questions or a report of misuse: open an issue at <https://github.com/rokrokss/recly>.
